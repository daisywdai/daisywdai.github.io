I am an Assistant Professor of Marketing at the Mitch Daniels School of Business, Purdue University. I study how information and pricing in consumer markets affect what consumers learn, which products and firms they choose, and who benefits. My work covers reviews, disclosure, advertising, and search on digital platforms, as well as pricing and consumer choice in health-related markets.

I received my PhD in economics from the University of Maryland.
